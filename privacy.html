Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 July 2026

Scope

This policy applies to one personal, self-hosted installation of Picsou, a personal-finance dashboard operated solely by its owner. It is not a public service.

Data processed

The installation may process account identifiers, balances, transactions, holdings, account-connection metadata and authentication tokens needed to display the owner’s financial information.

Purpose and storage

Data is processed only to aggregate, display and manage the owner’s personal financial information. The data is stored on the operator’s own local device and is not sold, rented or used for advertising.

Third parties

When the owner chooses to connect a bank account, Enable Banking and the relevant bank process the authorization flow and provide account information under their own terms and privacy policies. No bank credentials are collected by this website.

Retention and security

Data remains in the local installation until the owner removes it. The operator uses access controls, HTTPS and encrypted storage for supported secrets, but no system can guarantee absolute security.

Your rights and contact

As this is a personal installation, the operator controls the data and can access, correct or delete it directly. For privacy questions, use the contact email displayed during the Enable Banking authorization flow.
